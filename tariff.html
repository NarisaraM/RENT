Customer storage rates and how charges are calculated

How it is calculated (Charge sheet in COST-RENT.xlsx)
  • Days in terminal = ETD − gate-in date + 1
  • Customer free time: GP/OOG/TK/FLEXIBAG 5 days · DG/RF 3 days · RF at PAT (Terminal 1 / 2) 4 days · PAT with CGO TERM CFS/CY: 1 day · a value entered in Free Time replaces it
  • Chargeable days = days in terminal − free time
  • Storage charge = the daily rate for each chargeable day, by band: days 1–4, 5–8 and 9 onward
  • RF containers add an electric charge = days beyond 3 × daily rate (3 days everywhere, including PAT where storage free time is 4 days)
